Cents

An offline-first personal finance tracker for iPhone. Your records live on your device, in a database only you can read. There is no account, no server, and nothing to sign up for.

Support

Email aaabolfadl@gmail.com with anything at all — a bug, a question, a feature you miss. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong, and you will get a reply.

Your data

Cents does not collect your data. It has no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising identifier. The only outbound request it ever makes is for exchange rates, and that request contains a currency code and nothing else. You can turn it off entirely and enter rates by hand.

Because everything lives on your device, keeping a backup is on you. Settings → Backup writes a file you can store wherever you like.

Donations

Every feature of Cents is free, for everyone, forever. Donations unlock nothing — they are a thank you, and half of every one is passed on to charity.
